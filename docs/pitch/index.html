Diagnosis

Why it stalled: the design, not the idea

A tree walk at every hop

IRS walked the whole supply chain for each question. Every hop meant discovery, a contract negotiation, a registry lookup and a submodel read — in asynchronous jobs.

A heavy, bundled stack

The app came with its own IRS and the full infrastructure around it. Hard to run, hard to adopt next to what a company already operates.

Brittle to protocol change

The 26.09 release notes list IRS as not compatible with Saturn connector policies, tested only on EDC 0.12.2 with Jupiter policies.

Tractus-X 24.03, 24.05 and 24.08 release notes: “No execution of Load-, Performance- or Penetration Tests.”

Source: tractus-x-release CHANGELOG · Catena-X Traceability · 5

This is the slide to slow down on. Left: one question triggers a recursive walk; the cost grows with the depth of the chain and every hop is a full dataspace round trip. Each company answers its own level from a local cache and decides to forward — exactly the hop-by-hop model the Traceability KIT describes for alerts and investigations.

Our approach

Four design choices

Infrastructure is a prerequisite

Every participant already runs an EDC, a Digital Twin Registry and a wallet. The app uses them; it does not ship them.

One level across companies

Only direct partners are read. Deeper levels travel through notifications, hop by hop. No IRS.

Negotiate once, reuse

Contract agreements and access tokens are kept per partner and asset; twins live in a local cache.

Read every version in use

SerialPart, Batch, BoM and Usage in v3 and v4 map to one internal model. Partners upgrade at their own pace.

Catena-X Traceability · 7

Each choice answers one of the reasons Trace-X stalled. Prerequisite infrastructure answers the heavy bundled stack. One level answers the recursive walk. Negotiate-once answers the per-request round trips: in a local test against a production EDC, 303 twins sharing a handful of assets synced in 7.5 minutes through the EDC. Tolerant reading answers version churn: the network does not upgrade in lockstep.

Catena-X Traceability · 8

The app is the only new component. The EDC, the registry, the submodel server and the identity are what every participant runs anyway. All dataspace access goes through the official Tractus-X SDK behind our own interfaces, so moving to the next release (26.09: EDC 0.13) is a configuration and adapter change, not a rewrite. Setting it up takes an admin sign-in and a guided checklist on the Setup page: connector, registry, IdP, discovery and the company's own root CA, each with a connection test.
