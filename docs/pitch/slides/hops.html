This is the slide to slow down on. Left: one question triggers a recursive walk; the cost grows with the depth of the chain and every hop is a full dataspace round trip. Right: nobody walks the chain. Each company answers its own level from a local cache and decides to forward — exactly the hop-by-hop model the Traceability KIT describes for alerts and investigations.
