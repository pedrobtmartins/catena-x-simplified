The problem

When a part fails, who else is affected?

01

Which of my products contain it?

Narrow the scope to the exact serial numbers and batches — not a whole model year.

02

Which customers must I warn?

A quality alert goes downstream, to the direct customers that received the parts.

03

Which supplier must investigate?

A quality investigation goes upstream, to the direct supplier of the suspect component.

Catena-X answers this across companies — without anyone handing over their full bill of materials.

Catena-X Traceability · 2

This is the business question behind the Traceability KIT: precise recall scope instead of broad recalls. The answer is spread over many companies; each knows only its own level. Alerts go to customers, investigations to suppliers — that is the KIT's notification model.
