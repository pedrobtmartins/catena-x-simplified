Our approach

Four design choices

Infrastructure is a prerequisite

Every participant already runs an EDC, a Digital Twin Registry and a wallet. The app uses them; it does not ship them.

One level across companies

Only direct partners are read. Deeper levels travel through notifications, hop by hop. No IRS.

Negotiate once, reuse

Agreements and tokens are reused per partner and asset; twins live in a local cache, read again only when stale.

Read every version in use

SerialPart, Batch, BoM and Usage in v3 and v4 map to one internal model. Partners upgrade at their own pace.

Catena-X Traceability · 7

Each choice answers one of the reasons Trace-X stalled. Prerequisite infrastructure answers the heavy bundled stack. One level answers the recursive walk. Negotiate-once answers the per-request round trips: in a local test against a production EDC, 303 twins sharing a handful of assets synced in 7.5 minutes through the EDC. Partner syncs now read several partners and twins at once, retry passing failures, skip twins read in the last day and leave alone for a while a partner that keeps failing. Tolerant reading answers version churn: the network does not upgrade in lockstep.
